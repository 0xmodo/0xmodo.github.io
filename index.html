03 / ABOUT ME

Curiosity is the common thread.

I have an M.S. in Computer Science and work as a developer at a software company. This is my personal corner of the web — a place for independent projects and the subjects I want to understand better.

I enjoy frontend work because the details are tangible: a drawing gesture, a clear interaction, a little motion. Architecture asks a different set of questions: what belongs together, where state should live, and which trade-offs are worth making.

My next step is to connect those interests with a deeper understanding of databases. Building a small version of something is how I want to explore the decisions behind it.
